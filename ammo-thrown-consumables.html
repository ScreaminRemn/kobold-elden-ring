Thrown Consumables

Changes to thrown consumables.

  • −All pots can only have 1 of that type crafted at a time (Can only have 1 Fire Pot at a time, cannot have 2 Fire Pots)
  • +All pots (regular, roped, hefty) are now no longer consumable and, as such, are now infinite. TLDR, this literally makes all pots infinite.
  • +All pots have slightly increased tracking
  • =Just like with spells, the now “basic” pots now regenerate FP while more “complex” pots now cost FP
  • −All pots have 30% less damage
  • +All pots have 30% increased scaling

Throwing Dagger

  • +DEX scaling increased by 30%

Bone Dart

  • +STR scaling increased to match DEX scaling

Poisonbone Dart

  • −STR scaling removed
  • −DEX scaling removed
  • +ARC scaling increased to match the DEX scaling of Throwing Dagger

Kukri

  • +STR scaling increased by 80%
  • −DEX scaling decreased by 80%

Crystal Darts

  • −STR scaling removed
  • −DEX scaling removed
  • +INT scaling increased to match the DEX scaling of Throwing Dagger

Fan Daggers

  • −STR scaling decreased by 35%
  • −DEX scaling decreased by 35%
  • +added low BLEED build-up

Hefty Rot Pot

  • +applies a 20% FIRE damage absorption debuff on targets instead of 15%
