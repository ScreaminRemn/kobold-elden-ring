Armor Weights

The current light, medium, and heavy armor classifications.

Download v0.8.2Releases

All armor sets have been assigned 1 of 3 weight classes. These classes are: Light, Medium, and Heavy, in accordance to the player’s weight class. All “light” sets are 30 and under, all “medium” sets are 30.1 to 70, and all “heavy” sets are 70.1 to 100 to fit within the base 100 weight limit. Additionally, all armor sets have a defense profile based on what class they are in. Light armor has 5-15%, medium armor has 15.1-26%, and heavy has 26.1-40%.
